Hardwood 88 ("the game", "we", "us") is a basketball franchise game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The short version: you never make an account, we never ask for your name or email, and your franchise lives on your phone.

Information stored on your device

Your franchise saves (up to three slots: teams, players, settings, purchases unlocked) are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and ICLOUD BACKUP is on in the game's Settings (it is on by default), a copy of each save slot is stored in your private iCloud account so you can restore it on another device. You can turn the backup off at any time. We cannot see your iCloud data.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or microphone.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • Player names you type in the game stay in your save file.

Purchases

Purchases are one-time and charged to your Apple Account by Apple. There are no subscriptions.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad tracking in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Change ad consent (EEA/UK) from the game's Settings screen.
  • Delete the app to remove all local data; delete iCloud data in iPhone Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
